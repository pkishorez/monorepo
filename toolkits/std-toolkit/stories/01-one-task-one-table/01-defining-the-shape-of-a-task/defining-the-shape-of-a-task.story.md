The board is empty and nothing exists yet, not even the idea of a task. Before anything can be saved, the toolkit needs to know what a task is: which fields it has, what each one holds, and which one tells two tasks apart. This chapter writes that down once, and every later chapter builds on it.

A task here has a board it belongs to, a title, a status that is either open or done, someone it may be assigned to, a colour, and free-text notes. That description is the only place the shape of a task lives.
