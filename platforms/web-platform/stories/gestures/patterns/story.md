# Patterns

Drop in a sidebar you swipe open and a list you pull to refresh, already tuned to feel right.

Some touch behaviours every app wants, and each has fiddly details: resistance, how far is far enough, where it settles. The patterns are hooks that give you those details done.

`usePullToRefresh` makes [pulling the list down refresh it](web-platform/gestures/patterns/pull-to-refresh): it pulls with resistance, arms past a distance and holds while your refresh runs. `useSidebar` makes [the sidebar follow a swipe open and a swipe shut](web-platform/gestures/patterns/swipe-the-sidebar-open-and-shut), settling wherever the finger's speed would carry it.
