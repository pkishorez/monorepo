# Feedback

Let people hear and feel the moment a gesture will go through, before they let go.

A swipe that will commit should say so. Your swipe tells you as it crosses the line, and you answer with a short sound from `play` and a buzz on phones that have one.

[Sliding to pay clicks and buzzes as it arms](web-platform/gestures/feedback/slide-to-pay-clicks-and-buzzes), then confirms when the finger lifts.
