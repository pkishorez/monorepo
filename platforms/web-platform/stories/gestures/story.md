# Gestures

Let fingers swipe, drag, pinch and pick on your page, each in its own area, without breaking scrolling or clicks.

Each area of the screen that wants touch is a zone, and inside it you say what you are listening for. Anything you don't take goes back to the browser, so pages still scroll and buttons still click.

Most touch UI is a [swipe](web-platform/gestures/swipes): a row to delete, tabs to turn, a sheet from the bottom edge. When you need the finger itself, you can follow it to [drag something or tell a tap from a long press](web-platform/gestures/drag-and-press), and follow two fingers to [pinch and turn](web-platform/gestures/pinch-and-turn).

For the things every app has, [ready-made patterns](web-platform/gestures/patterns) give you a sidebar and pull to refresh. When areas sit inside each other, [zones](web-platform/gestures/zones) decide which one gets a touch.

A swipe can [click and buzz](web-platform/gestures/feedback) as it arms, so people feel it before they let go. And with two fingers, the [Thumb Picker](web-platform/gestures/thumb-picker) lets someone pick from a deep menu without lifting their hand.
