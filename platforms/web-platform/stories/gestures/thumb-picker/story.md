# Thumb Picker

Pick from a deep menu with one hand: one finger rests, another swipes through the choices, and lifting picks.

Deep menus on a phone mean tapping through screen after screen. With `ThumbPicker`, a thumb rests and another finger walks the menu: down and up move through a list, right opens a choice, left goes back. Each move ticks and buzzes.

[A resting thumb and a swiping finger pick a place](web-platform/gestures/thumb-picker/thumb-and-finger-pick-a-place). Mistakes are forgiving: [a move that leads nowhere shakes the menu once](web-platform/gestures/thumb-picker/a-wrong-way-shakes-once) and the walk goes on, and [lifting the thumb first picks nothing](web-platform/gestures/thumb-picker/lifting-the-thumb-first-picks-nothing), so you can always back out.
