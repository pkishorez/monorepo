# Pinch and turn

Follow two fingers at once to zoom and rotate, the way a photo app does.

With two fingers down, `useGesture` follows each one on its own, so you can read how far apart they are and the angle between them.

The spread lets you [pinch a photo to zoom it](web-platform/gestures/pinch-and-turn/pinch-to-zoom). The angle lets you [turn a dial](web-platform/gestures/pinch-and-turn/turn-a-dial), and it keeps counting past a half turn. Both come from the same two fingers, so you can [zoom and turn a sticker in one move](web-platform/gestures/pinch-and-turn/zoom-and-turn-in-one-move).
