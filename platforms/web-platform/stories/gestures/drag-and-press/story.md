# Drag and press

Follow one finger exactly, to drag something across the screen or tell a tap from a long press.

Some touches aren't swipes. A card should stay under the finger wherever it goes, and a photo should open on a tap but show a menu when held. `useGesture` gives you every finger in your zone as values that follow it, and leaves the meaning to you.

[One finger drags a card to another column](web-platform/gestures/drag-and-press/drag-a-card), and where it lifts picks the column. Timing the same touch tells [a tap from a long press](web-platform/gestures/drag-and-press/tap-or-long-press).
