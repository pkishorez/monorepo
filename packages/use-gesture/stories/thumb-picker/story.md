# Thumb Lock and Tree Walk

Rest a thumb, move one finger, and pick from a whole tree of choices without lifting your hand.

A long menu on a phone means scrolling and tapping small targets. With a thumb resting on the screen, another finger can walk the menu instead: down and up move through a list, right opens a choice, left goes back, and lifting the finger chooses.

`thumbLock` listens to your provider and tells you when a thumb rests beside a moving finger and how that finger moves. `TreeWalk` turns those moves into a place in your tree of choices, and tells you which lists to draw.

Lifting the finger [files a receipt in the folder it reached](use-gesture/thumb-picker/a-thumb-and-a-finger-file-a-receipt).
