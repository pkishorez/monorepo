`recipes/effect-oak-devtools` is rebuilt. `EffectOakDevtools` takes only the app's runtime, `App.useRuntime()`, and fills whatever holds it: it no longer takes the app as `children` or docks beside it, and its Timeline, Inspector and panel are replaced by one Inspector on the running app. Its Timeline is the app's History, a Step picked there Time Travels the app itself, and live, each Worker's keys say what they are doing. `Inspector` draws one Step of an Effect Oak app as a Map, from Snapshots given to it: one card per place an Actor runs, its size from the Actor alone, laid out as a packed tree or an outline. Each card shows the Actor's name, its States as options with the one its Instance is in filled, and a row for every Worker it has, one dot per key running, breathing while live and red where one failed. A slot of keyed Children is one stacked card that shows one Instance at a time, with a switcher to step through them: its States count every Instance and mark the shown one's, and only the shown one's Children hang below it; picking it opens its Instances beside it. A right click on a card folds what hangs below it into a row of names under it. A card where something runs is raised with a shadow; one where nothing does lies flat. Under a card, a small node per State that starts something: the State it is in, lit, holds what it starts; any other is faded and names what it would start; what runs in every State hangs from the card itself. A Transition moves the Map: what stops shrinks into what it hung from, what starts grows out of it, and the rest glides to its new place. Links are smooth curves. Picking a card keeps it, its way up to the root and what hangs below it bright while the rest fades; a tap on the empty Map lets go. The Step's Message sits on the edges of the card it went to, which glows: who Sent it on top and the Commands asked for below, with an arrow from exactly what Sent it, the Worker's row or the Sender's name, into that card. What is picked opens in a Sheet: a section under the Map on a wide Inspector, a full-width drawer on a narrow one. The Timeline under it picks the Step shown, and a corner mark says whether it is live. It pans by a drag from anywhere, over a card too, and zooms by scroll or pinch; a click anywhere on a card picks it, and a right click never opens the browser's menu. It lays itself out by its own width. `JsonTree` takes `tone="syntax"` to colour values by kind. `effect-oak` is an optional peer, needed only by this recipe.
