Pickers follow the input that opened them, not the device: keys a centred list, a mouse a popover by its button, a touch a Drawer or a Dialog at the top with nothing focused; the screen dims behind each. No focus rings on touch. The Draft on a device with no keyboard is the Touch Draft, its own actor: one microphone at the bottom right with Send over it once there is text, which a tap or a swipe of the microphone up onto it sends; while a Dictation records the microphone takes it, a swipe up takes it and sends once it is text, and a small cancel throws it away. Left of it, two lines of the Draft, edit, which makes the card the Composer above the keyboard with Done, the model chip, Skills and Quick actions: ask on the side, paste, attach a file or a photo, and the microphone.
