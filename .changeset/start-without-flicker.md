A signed-in reload opens straight from what this device kept: the mark shows only on a start slower than a second, "Checking who is signed in…" only when no Account is remembered and the answer is slow, and Frame draws at once, handed its keyboard by the root page, which reads Preferences once, in one transaction. Switching Account, signing one out, or finding another active keeps the Account drawn on screen until the next one's Machines have read their kept list, then draws it, home, in one step.

`browser`'s `cache` remembers the Accounts last signed in, so `state` starts `Checking { last }` even after a signed-out answer, such as an Auth Worker's while it restarts. A sign-out asked here (`signOut` of the last Account, `signOutAll`), or a signed-out answer to the check made while they are shown, forgets them. A cache that cannot be read or written says so as a warning instead of failing silently.
