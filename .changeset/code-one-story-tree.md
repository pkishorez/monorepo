The sidebar keeps every session it shows in sync ahead of time, so opening one shows its messages at once, and it holds only their ids while it waits. Dictation works from anywhere: start it from any page, Settings included, by its keys or the new mic in the header, keep moving while it records, and when it stops the text goes into the Draft on screen; with no Draft on screen it waits in a dictation list in the corner, to paste into a Draft later or throw away. A side question is asked through its own toggle in the Draft, not by typing `/btw`. A skill shows as a chip once picked from the Skills Picker, not because its name was typed. On a phone, picking a project opens its new-session page, as on a wide screen: the Starter drawer is gone. The Actions list shows each action's keys, in place of a separate keys picker. A new project's page says again how to start its first session.
