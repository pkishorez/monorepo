The transcript follows what you send; your messages, tool calls and images are easier to handle; and Actions regenerates a title without waiting.

- Sending a message, typed or dictated, takes the transcript back to the bottom, so it shows as it arrives.
- Each of your messages has a Copy button under it. One taller than 300px is folded, its foot fading out; a click on it, or Show more, opens it, and again folds it.
- A line of work, such as "Ran 3 commands", opens from anywhere across its width. Open, its line, and that of each tool call or subagent opened in it, sticks to the top while what it opened scrolls under it, so it can be shut from there without the page jumping.
- An image opens full screen to zoom into: pinch, double tap or the wheel zooms it, a drag moves it, and a swipe down closes it; on a desktop, buttons and `+`, `-`, `0` zoom too.
- Regenerate the title closes Actions at once: the header's title pulses until the new one is written, and a refusal shows as a toast.
- Every action on the open session has a line under it saying what it does.
