Story cards start folded: only the model's top-level keys show, and each section, State and nested key shows its count until opened. A Beat's lit refs open what holds them, the whole section with the lit rows marked and the rest dimmed. `Folded` opens or closes any part of a card by its fold, as `state:Ready`, `messages`, `state:Ready/workers` or `model.settings`.
