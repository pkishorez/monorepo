The Devtools Dock hides its devtools when closed instead of taking them down, so what was chosen in them is still there when they open again. They are drawn from the first open on; a new Layout still lays them out afresh.

A small card in Kabuki Devtools gives the key it is made again by a line of its own, made readable: a UUID by its first eight characters, a long id by its start and end, a list of ids by its parts in order, the whole on hover. Its name and State keep the first line to themselves.

The button that folds and unfolds what is under a card is larger, with a reach past its edge, and sits beside the card, clear of what it says, at its middle, where its lines now leave and arrive.
