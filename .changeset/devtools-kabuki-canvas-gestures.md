The Inspector's canvas zooms only with Cmd or Ctrl and the wheel, or a pinch; a plain wheel scrolls the box under the pointer when it can scroll, and otherwise does nothing. A drag pans from anywhere on a card, its Actions included, except inside a text field. An open card's Actions are one more fold beside Model and Props, closed until opened; on a past Step, "Go back to now" stays outside it. An open card always shows all its States and its Services, so its header has no switches for them. A State's object and list values show as a JSON tree, like the Model, instead of a count; values are drawn at 13px.
