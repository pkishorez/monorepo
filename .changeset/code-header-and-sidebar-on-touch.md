On a touch screen a session row in the sidebar swipes left to right to Settle it, and a Settled one to un-Settle it, with a quiet "Settle" or "Keep" behind it as it goes: it slides out, the list closes up and a toast offers Undo for five seconds, and only once those have passed, or the page is hidden, is its Machine asked, so an Undo asks nothing and the row never moves back. Each session waits and is asked on its own, one call at a time, and the Settled List shows the last swiped first. Pulling the sidebar down from its top Reloads the Machines, with a small spinner that follows the pull and holds still under reduced motion; scrolled down, a pull scrolls the list, a session row included. With no keyboard a Settled row's Un-settle button is there for a screen reader only, and with one, a touch screen's included, it shows as before. On a phone the header is the back arrow, where you are, the offline mark in a word and one finger-wide `⋯` for Session Actions; with no keyboard, at any width, session rows are 48px tall, other rows, the toast's Undo, the header's `⋯` and project link, Reload, the theme switch, the Project Picker's `+` with a heading tall enough for it, the Settled List's close, the transcript's "Earlier in" and fork links, and Settings' buttons, fields and switch 44px to tap, the harness path field in 16px text, the foot no longer doubles the home indicator's space, and the Machine's name heads the sidebar. The home page's "Open a folder" works out of Vim Mode too, as with no keyboard, where it had been disabled. With a keyboard nothing changes.
