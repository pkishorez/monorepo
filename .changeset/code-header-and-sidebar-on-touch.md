On a touch screen a session row in the sidebar swipes left to right to Settle it, and a Settled one to un-Settle it, with a quiet "Settle" or "Keep" behind it as it goes: it slides out, the list closes up and a toast offers Undo for five seconds, while its Machine is asked in the order swiped. Pulling the sidebar down from its top Reloads the Machines, with a small spinner that follows the pull. On a phone the header is the back arrow, where you are, the offline mark in a word and one finger-wide `⋯` for Session Actions; session rows are 48px tall, the foot no longer doubles the home indicator's space, and the Machine's name heads the sidebar. With a keyboard nothing changes.
