The Runtime, and an App's `useRuntime()`, say what an Instance provides now: `provided(id)` gives the keys of the Capabilities its top and its State built for everything under it, as `workers(id)` says what its Workers are doing.

A built Actor's definition keeps the Schemas it was defined with, under `schemas`, so a tool can say what its Input, Props, Model, State and Messages are.
