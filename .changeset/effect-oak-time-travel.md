Every Envelope carries its Source: the View, Command or Lifetime that Sent it, and its Instance, so a Request shows where it came from. `snapshotAt` gives the Snapshot right after any entry, `show('init')` draws the app right after init, `clear` forgets every Branch and goes live from init, an Actor's definition lists its `states`, and `AppRuntime.definition` gives the root's.
