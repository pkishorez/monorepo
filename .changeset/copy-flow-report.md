Add a **Copy Flow** button to the Lotel Flow view. It copies the whole Recorded Flow as a Markdown report: a summary, every Participant with its Activations, every Message with its Reply latency, the chronological timeline with attributes and Activity logs, and any warnings. Paste it into a bug report or hand it to an agent to explain what went wrong without opening DevTools.
