The app signs in and works on your Machines. Signed out, it shows only a way to sign in, through the Auth Worker or, under `dev:local`, by typing an email. The Machine Switcher sits where the logo was, names the Machine you are on and lists the rest. Every place is now on one Machine, under `/m/:machineId`, and that page opens the Machine's own socket and its own records, so switching Machine closes the old ones. At `/` the app opens the Machine last used on this device, else the one seen last; with none, it says to run `npx @kestack/ai-toolkit serve`. The list of Machines is kept per Account on this device, so the app reopens even while the Backend is down, and is asked again every 15 seconds while the page is visible. While a Machine is out of reach, what it synced stays readable and the header says why: still connecting, its tunnel broken, or the Machine down, with when it was last seen. Settings → Machines lists each Machine with when it was last seen and how its tunnel stands. Forget asks first and then deletes the Machine's records on this device, and signing an Account out deletes the records of its Machines. Unseen marks are kept per Machine; marks from before Machines are dropped.
