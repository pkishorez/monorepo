What a native app's lists and forms need, from Ledger's Places:

- `@kstackz/expo-platform/components/choice`: `Choice`, one of a few things as a row of pills that scrolls sideways, and can reach past its container to the screen's edge (`bleed`).
- `@kstackz/expo-platform/components/meter`: `Meter`, a thin bar of how full something is, with an optional limit mark.
- `@kstackz/expo-platform/patterns/sheet`: `Sheet`, a sheet from the bottom for a short form, kept above the keyboard.
- `@kstackz/expo-platform/patterns/swipe-row`: `SwipeRow`, a row swiped left to delete over Panel UI's `Swipe`.
