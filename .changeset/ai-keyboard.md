The app has a keyboard, from `@kestack/use-keys`, defined in one typed file. `Mod+Alt+V` or a switch in Settings turns Vim Mode on, which this browser keeps; the app then floats as a window with a status line that says where you are, and `?` lists every key. You work in two Surfaces, the whole sidebar and the whole page, its header and composer included, lit while Active and dimmed while not. `Ctrl+H` or `Space e`, and `Ctrl+L`, move between them. The sidebar is a tree: each project folds and unfolds, by its chevron or by `h` and `l`, and the project open is unfolded; `j`/`k`, `g g`, `Shift+G` move through the rows shown and Enter opens one and moves the keyboard to it. A folded project with a session at work shows it on its row. In a session `j`/`k` and `Ctrl+D`/`Ctrl+U` scroll the transcript, `i` writes in the composer, whose border lights up while you type, and Escape leaves it for the page; `Ctrl+[` and `Ctrl+]` open the previous and next session in the sidebar's order, even while typing. Always, Page Up and Page Down scroll the transcript even while typing, `Mod+.` stops the running turn, and `Mod+Shift+M` opens the model picker. The skills palette, the model picker and the help sheet are open exactly while their Surface is Active: Escape clears the search first, then closes, back where they were opened; the skills palette gives the editor its focus back. Escape closes a `/btw` answer.
