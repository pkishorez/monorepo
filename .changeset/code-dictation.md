Dictation: Space, from the page or the sidebar, or Ctrl+Space anywhere, the Composer and every Picker included, starts recording and stops it, and so does the mic button in the Status Line. The browser records raw PCM through an AudioWorklet, one channel at 16 kHz, and keeps the whole clip; it stops itself at five minutes. While it records, a mic badge in the bottom right corner, over Pickers, dialogs and Settings, shows the time and a level, and it carries on while you move between sessions, Pickers and Settings. When it stops, a "transcribing…" placeholder sits at the cursor of the Draft open then, until its text goes in there as any insert does, highlighted, and `u` or Mod+Z takes it out. It is written out on the open session's Machine when that one has Speech to Text ready and answers, else on any Machine that does; refused, the placeholder becomes "couldn't transcribe — retry", which tries the same clip again. When it cannot start, because the microphone is blocked or missing or no Machine has Speech to Text on, the badge says why for a few seconds. Escape never touches a Dictation. Settings shows each Machine's Speech to Text switch with its live state: off, downloading with how much of the model is in, ready, or failed with a retry; off deletes the model.
