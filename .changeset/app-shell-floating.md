The App Shell floats as a window only when given `floating`; Vim Mode alone no longer floats it, so by default it fills the screen at every width. Floating in or out now eases rather than jumping. The sidebar's resize handle sits over the line between the sidebar and the page, found from either side, and no longer shows a tooltip.

The App Shell's header drops the divider after the sidebar's button, and shows its bottom edge only once the page is scrolled under it.
