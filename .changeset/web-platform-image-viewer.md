`./recipes/image-viewer` shows an image whole over the page to zoom into: `ImageViewer`, opened on an image by its caller, and `ImageButton`, a thumbnail that opens it when pressed. Pinch, a double tap, the wheel, its buttons or `+`, `-` and `0` zoom it, a drag moves it, and a swipe down, Escape or a tap on the backdrop close it at once.
