A Drawer can be swiped open from the edge of the screen: `DrawerSwipeArea`, from `@kestack/web-platform/ui/components/drawer`, is an invisible strip you place along an edge, and swiping away from it pulls the Drawer out under the finger, the way a side menu opens on a phone. The page still scrolls the other way through it.
