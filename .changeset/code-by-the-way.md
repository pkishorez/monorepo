Side questions are now **By the way**, asked from the composer and answered in a drawer, while the transcript stays where it is.

- The `btw` button, first on the composer's status line, turns the composer blue and into a By the way composer: the agent's own answer, no files or model, and Send says Ask. On a phone it is the first row of Add, and the collapsed Draft shows the `btw` mark.
- Asking opens the answer in a read-only drawer that rises from behind the composer over the foot of the transcript, with Copy, Minimise and Close. On a phone it is a sheet that a swipe down minimises.
- Minimised, the drawer is a bar on the composer with the question and how it stands; tapping it opens it again. Closing it drops the question and its answer.
- Ctrl+Q, or `q` in Vim Mode, brings By the way forward a step: into the mode with its last answer open, the answer open again, then back to the message. Escape goes back a step: the drawer minimised, then the message.
- The transcript is never swapped out for the answer any more, and the agent keeps working while it answers.
