Ctrl+H and Ctrl+L go to the sidebar and the page, Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] open the session before and after in the sidebar, as `[` and `]` do, both now going round past either end, and Ctrl+O and Ctrl+I go Back and Forward, from anywhere, the Composer included. Back and Forward put every scroll back where it was on that page, a session's transcript too, even as its messages are still being read; a page newly opened starts at the top, and a session at its bottom. Held down, `[`, `]`, Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] keep stepping through the sessions.
