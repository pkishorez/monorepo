Monoverse shows where a Package's rules bend, which folders are leaves, and one folder at a time.

**Added**

- An import a door or an exception lets through is an Exception: drawn violet, and counted on its file, every folder above it and the Package, beside the red Violation count. The selection's count lists which door or exception lets each one through.
- The view settings choose which Connections are drawn: Violations, Exceptions and Allowed, all on at first.
- A folder other folders of its Package import, and that imports none of them, is marked as a leaf.
- Double-clicking a folder, or Show alone on its selection (the way on a phone), shows it alone, in a Package or the repo view, with a trail above the tree back up; on a phone the trail folds its middle into one step up. The folders and files outside it that it connects to are drawn dimmed; the Outside folders setting hides them.

**Changed**

- Double-clicking a folder, or Enter on it, shows it alone instead of opening it. Its chevron and the arrow keys still open and close it.
