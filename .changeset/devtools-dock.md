New `recipes/devtools-dock`: `DevtoolsDock` wraps a page with devtools opened from a fixed corner button or Ctrl+/, laid out full screen, as a resizable bar or side panel, or as a floating window, kept across reloads. A click on the Layout button steps to the next Layout, a right click picks one; phones get only full screen and the bar. The Kabuki Timeline keeps clear of the button, the Sheet is a panel over the Map, and the History's Window is a Base UI select with presets from 1 to 500.
