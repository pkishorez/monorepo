The Kabuki Tool shows an open Kabuki Session as its Inspector or its Story. Story asks the Agent about the app's code in a Worktree of its Project, both picked once and remembered per app: the Project from the Project registry, then any of its Worktrees by branch (or "detached @" and its HEAD), the primary first and the current one by default. Monoverse's Server answers a new `GetWorktrees` call for it. Whatever the question, the Agent answers with a story told on the app's Blueprint, one Beat at a time, in Kabuki's levels: the actor and what it is for, its model keys, the State it starts in, what each State runs and takes, the Messages that move it between States, then its Children and how they talk. Every card has the same shape: its description, its model keys, Always (the Workers, Messages and Children of the top level, then its Emits, Requires, Provides, Queries, Input and Props), then one row per State, collapsed to its name and a count until something in it is lit. A card names keys and payloads, never values or types; a key's description shows when it is lit. Each Beat is the whole picture on screen: the actor the camera centres on, every lit card with exactly the refs lit on it (`model.done`, `state:Loading`, `worker:load`, `message:Loaded.items`, `child:todo`, …), each found at its own level, and the links lit between actors. The rest of a lit card stays, dimmed; every other card is quiet, name only. Two cards have one connector at most: a link between a parent and its Child lights their edge and puts its label on it as a small chip marked ↓ or ↑, and only cards with no edge between them get a quiet curve of their own; edges follow the cards as they move. While a story plays or is stepped, the canvas follows its Beats; panning and zooming keep it so, and the next Beat pans to centre its actor at the zoom the person chose. Lighting a row, expanding a State or clicking a card leaves for Explore mode, from that Beat's picture, where any row lights and any State expands by hand, and adds its ref to the next question; "Back to story", a Beat in the chat, Back and Next return to it. Back, Next, Play and ← → step through the Beats; the chat lists each answer's numbered Beats, keeps the current one in view and marks what the Blueprint does not have as not found. While the Agent reads, its activity and the time taken show with Stop; a failed answer says why with Retry. Lotel's Preferences are now the app's, shared by every Tool. `@kestack/devtools-kabuki/devtools` also exports the Board's canvas (`useSpace`, which can now also centre a rect at the zoom it has, `unionOf`, `CARD_SPRING`) and the `Plan` Schema.
