`@kestack/ai-toolkit/speech/fake` gains `speech`, the Speech fake: Speech to Text on a computer as a Fake the Fake World runs, its download held, moved, finished or failed, and what a Take turns into (a text, words as they are heard, nothing, a failure, no answer) set by its Controls. `speechFakeOver` serves `Speech` with the real speech model and Takes over it: Speech's front door now runs over any `SpeechHost`, in a browser too. `makeFakeSpeech` no longer uses `node:fs`.
