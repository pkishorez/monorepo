# Summary rules

Claude reads this file and `learnings.md` when it writes a video's summary
from the trimmed transcript.

- Start with two or three sentences saying what the video is about and what
  a viewer leaves with.
- Then the key points, one line each, in the order the video makes them.
  Each points at the sentence where it is made, so it links to a time.
- Then chapters: a short plain title each, pointing at the sentence where the
  chapter starts. Use the creator's chapters when they still fit the short
  video; otherwise make new ones, one every few minutes of the short video.
- Say only what the video says. No outside facts, no advice of your own.
- Plain words. No hype, no "in this video we delve into".
