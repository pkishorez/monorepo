A list of products, a cart, and a checkout. The list and the cart are two
Children of one Shop, and they never talk to each other. Add a few things,
change your mind, and check out. Open the **Devtools** from the window's
bar to watch every change climb from a Child to the Shop as a Message.

## How it works

The Shop is one [Actor](/kabuki/actor) that keeps the order: a list of
lines, each a product and how many. It has two
[States](/kabuki/state-machine): `Shopping`, and `Placed` once you check
out.

While it's `Shopping`, it has [Children](/kabuki/children): a `Product`
for each thing it sells, and one `Cart`. It hands them what they need as
[Props](/kabuki/props). Each Product gets how many of it are in the cart.
The Cart gets the lines. Neither keeps a copy.

A Child can't change its parent's Model. So the Shop provides a
[Capability](/kabuki/capabilities), the `Basket`, with three things to ask
for: add, change, and check out. A Product's Add button sends it a Message,
and its Update runs a Command that asks the Basket. The Basket turns the
ask into a Message to the Shop. The Shop's Update changes the lines, and
the new Props flow back down to every Child.

The Children don't know who provides the Basket. Put them under another
Actor that provides one, and they work the same.

## Things to try

Open the **Devtools** from the window's bar first. They show the Timeline
of every Step, and under it the Map of the app's Actors.

- **Follow one press up the tree.** Add a mug. On the Map, the Product's
  card flashes with `Added`, then the Shop's card flashes with its own
  `Added`. Click the Shop's card to see the lines in its Model.
- **Go back in time.** Add, change and check out, then click or drag along
  the Timeline. The cart fills and empties as it was, read only. Go live
  to come back.
- **Change the past.** Go back to a Step before you checked out, press
  Fork, and add something else. The new order grows a new Branch; the one
  you placed is still there.
