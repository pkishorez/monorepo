A stopwatch: start it, take laps, pause, resume, reset. While it runs, a
Worker sends a Tick once a second. When it stops running, so does the
Worker. Start it, and watch the devtools under the app.

<AppWindow app={StopwatchApp} url="stopwatch.app" />

## How it works

The Stopwatch is one Actor with three [States](/kabuki/state-machine):
`Idle`, `Running` and `Paused`. Each State takes only the Messages that make
sense in it: you can take a lap only while it runs, and reset only once it
is paused.

The `tick` [Worker](/kabuki/workers) is written inside `Running`, so it runs
only there. Nothing starts or stops it: entering `Running` starts it, and
leaving stops it. Each `Ticked` banks the time so far into the Model and stays
in `Running`, so the Worker keeps going.

Every Update is handed `at`, the time its Message arrived. So `Paused` and
`Lapped` are exact to the hundredth, with no Tick needed. The Worker could
tick every 10ms, but then every second would add a hundred Steps to the
History. Once a second keeps the History short, and the View counts the
hundredths on screen in between. That count is only drawing: the Actor never
sees it, and it stands still when you look at a past Step.

## Things to try

- **Watch the Worker come and go.** Press Start. On the Map, the Stopwatch's
  card shows `tick` running, and a `Ticked` arrives each second. Press Pause,
  and the Worker is gone. Click the card to see the Model and State.
- **Go back in time.** Take a few laps, pause, then click or drag along the
  Timeline. The time and laps show as they were at each Step, and the
  hundredths stop counting. Go live to come back.
- **Change the past.** Pick a Step from before a lap, fork, and press
  something else. The new Messages grow a new Branch, and the Worker starts
  again if that Step was `Running`.
