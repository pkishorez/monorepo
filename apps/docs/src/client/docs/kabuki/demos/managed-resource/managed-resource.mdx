A live price, and a switch for the feed behind it. Turn it on, and the feed
opens. Turn it off, and the feed closes. Nothing closes it by hand: leaving
the State does.

<AppWindow app={TickerApp} url="ticker.app" />

## How it works

The Ticker is one [Actor](/kabuki/actor) with two
[States](/kabuki/state-machine), `Off` and `On`. A `Switched` Message moves
it from one to the other.

The price comes from a `PriceFeed`, a [Capability](/kabuki/capabilities)
built from an Effect Layer. The Layer opens the feed when it is built, and
closes it when its Scope closes. Each time, it sends the Ticker a `Logged`
Message, so you can watch it happen.

Only the `On` State provides the feed. In the same State lives a Quote, a
[Child](/kabuki/children) that requires a `PriceFeed`. Its
[Worker](/kabuki/workers) reads a price every second and a half, and sends
it to the Quote as a `Priced` Message.

Switch off, and the Ticker leaves `On`. Everything that State started stops
with it: the Quote and its Worker first, then the feed, whose release runs
and logs `Feed closed`.

## Things to try

- **Watch it come and go.** Switch on. A Quote appears under the Ticker on
  the Map, and its Worker starts. Switch off, and both are gone. Click a
  card to see its Model and State.
- **Go back in time.** Click or drag along the Timeline to an earlier Step.
  The app shows the price as it was, read only. Nothing opens or closes
  while you look: a past Step only draws.
- **Fork while it's on.** From a Step in `On`, fork. The new Branch opens a
  fresh feed of its own, and logs it.
