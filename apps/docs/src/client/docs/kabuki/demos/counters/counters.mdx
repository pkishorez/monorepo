A list of Counters. Add one, remove one, and press each on its own. Every
Counter is an Actor of its own, a Child of the list, with its own count.
The total at the top adds them all up.

<Live app={CountersApp} url="counters.app" />

## How it works

The list is one [Actor](/kabuki/actor). Its Model is only a list of ids,
and it takes two Messages: `Added` and `Removed`. It never holds a count.

Each id is a [Child](/kabuki/children): a Counter, with its own Model,
`{ count }`, and its own Messages. The list doesn't create or stop them by
hand. It changes its ids, and Kabuki starts a Counter for each new id and
stops the one whose id is gone.

The list also hands each Counter its place, as a [Prop](/kabuki/props).
Remove the first Counter and the others move up: their places change,
because the list says so, but their counts stay, because those are their
own.

The total is read in the list's View. It looks at each Counter's count to
add them up, and can never change one.

## Things to try

Open the **Devtools** from the window's bar first. They show the Timeline
of every Step, and under it the Map of the app's Actors.

- **See the tree.** Add a Counter. On the Map, the Counter card under the
  list now counts one more; step through them with its arrows. Click it to
  see that Counter's Model and Props.
- **Keep what's yours.** Press a few times on Counter 2, then remove
  Counter 1. Counter 2 becomes Counter 1 and keeps its count. Click its
  card: the place Prop changed, the Model didn't.
- **Go back and fork.** Click or drag along the Timeline to a Step before a
  Counter was removed. It's back, with its count. Press Fork there and
  press something else: a new Branch grows, and the old one is still there.
