A whole app, opening: it loads its assets, waits for you to sign in, then
keeps a heartbeat, syncs and downloads files until you sign out. Every
part of it is an Actor. Open the **Devtools** from the window's bar to
see them drawn as they come and go. The reload in the address bar starts
it over.

## How it works

The app is the `App` Actor, and its [States](/kabuki/state-machine) are
the screens: `Booting`, `Failed`, `SignedOut` and `Inside`. Each State
only accepts its own Messages: `SignIn` means something while
`SignedOut`, and nothing anywhere else.

While `Booting`, its `load assets` [Worker](/kabuki/workers) loads the
fonts, the images and the sounds, one `AssetLoaded` each. When the last
one lands, the app is `SignedOut`, and the Worker is gone with the State
it ran in.

Sign in, and once the `sign in` Command answers, the app is `Inside`.
`Inside` Invokes two [Children](/kabuki/children): `Home`, with its
heartbeat and the sync you turn on and off, and `Downloads`, with a
`Download` of its own for every file. Sign out, and `Inside` ends: both
Children stop, and every Worker under them is interrupted. There is no
cleanup code: each Worker is an Effect, so stopping it is interrupting it.

`Home` signs out through a [Capability](/kabuki/capabilities), the
`Session`, that `Inside` Provides. It asks; the App decides.

The same app runs full screen, with the devtools over it, in the
[full-screen demo](/kabuki/demos/opening-an-app).

## Things to try

Open the **Devtools** from the window's bar first. They show the Timeline
of every Step, and under it the Map of the app's Actors.

- **Watch it boot.** Reload it from the address bar. On the Map, `App` is
  `Booting` and runs `load assets`; when the last asset lands, the Worker
  is gone.
- **Sign in.** The tree grows: `Home` and `Downloads` under `App`, and
  `Home`'s `heartbeat` beating every few seconds.
- **Add a file.** A `Download` card joins the tree for each one. It is
  `Downloading` and runs `download` until it is `Done`; cancel it, and its
  card goes.
- **Sign out.** Everything under `Inside` stops at once.
