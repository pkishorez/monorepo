A list of to-dos. Add one, check it off, rename it, delete it, filter the
list, and clear the ones that are done. The list is one Actor, and every
to-do is an Actor of its own. Watch the devtools under the app: a card for
the list, and the to-dos' cards under it.

<AppWindow app={TodosApp} url="todos.app" />

## How it works

The list is the `Todos` Actor. Its filter is its
[State](/kabuki/state-machine): `All`, `Active` or `Done`, and `Filtered`
moves it from one to the next. Its Model keeps what it acts on: which
to-dos there are, and which are done. So `Toggled`, `Removed` and
`ClearedDone` are its Messages.

Each to-do is a `Todo` [Child](/kabuki/children), one per id in the list.
Add an id and a Todo starts; remove one and only that Todo stops. A Todo
gets its text as Input, once, and from then on the text is its own.
Renaming it is a Message to that Todo alone.

Whether a to-do is done, the list decides, and hands it down as a
[Prop](/kabuki/props). The Todo only reads it, to strike its text through.

The text you type stays in React until you press Add. Only then does it
become a Message.

## Things to try

- **One card per to-do.** Under the list on the Map is a stack of Todo
  cards, one per to-do; its arrows step through them. Add a to-do and the
  stack grows. Check one off, then click its card: its Prop says `done`.
  Rename it, and the Message lands on that card, not the list's.
- **Go back in time.** Delete a to-do, then click or drag along the
  Timeline. It comes back, and its card with it. Go live to come back.
- **Change the past.** From an earlier Step, fork, and do something else.
  The new Messages grow a new Branch; the old one is still there.
