A number, a button to add one, a button to take one away, and a reset.
Every press is a Message to one Actor. Open the **Devtools** from the
window's bar and press a few: each Message arrives, and the count it left
behind.

## How it works

The Counter is one [Actor](/kabuki/actor). Its Model is `{ count }`, and it
takes three Messages: `Incremented`, `Decremented` and `Reset`. Its Update
says what each one does to the count. That's the whole app's logic, in a
dozen lines, and none of it knows React is there.

The [View](/kabuki/view) draws the count, and turns each button press into
a Message with `send`. It never changes the count itself; it asks, and the
Actor decides.

`View.app` turns the View into one React component, `<CounterApp />`, that you
can put anywhere.

## Things to try

Open the **Devtools** from the window's bar first. They show the Timeline
of every Step, and under it the Map of the app's Actors.

- **Watch a Message arrive.** Press `+`. The Counter's card on the Map
  flashes with the Message. Click the card to see its Model.
- **Go back in time.** Press a few times, then click or drag along the
  Timeline. The app shows the count as it was, read only. Go live to
  come back. This is the Counter's [History](/kabuki/history).
- **Change the past.** From an earlier Step, press Fork, and press
  something else. The new presses grow a new Branch; the old one is still
  there.
